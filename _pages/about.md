Urban environmental planner - Community engagement - Climate resilience * urban nature conservation - Urban games

I work at the nexus of science, policy, and community participation in urban planning so we can all live in, with, for and by nature. Urban planning policy researcher. I dabble in spatial and data analysis for biodiversity conservation at the peri-urban boundary.

Previously working with the public space team at UN-Habitat to publish several reports including "Healthier Cities and Communities through Public Spaces", "The Critical Role of Nature-based Solutions for Enhancing Climate Resilience in Informal Areas: An Urban Supplement to the UNFCCC Technical Guidelines on National Adaptation Plans", and "La ciudad como sistema: metabolismo, resiliencia, y sustentabilidad".
